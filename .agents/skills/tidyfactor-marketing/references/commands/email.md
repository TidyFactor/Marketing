# Command: email

Runtime dispatcher for Email List Growth, Automated Welcome Sequences, Abandoned Cart Recovery, and Win-Back Campaigns.

---

## Capabilities Handled

1. **Grow My Email List**: High-converting lead magnet mechanics, exit-intent popups, 2-step opt-in copy.
2. **Welcome Email Sequence**: 5-part onboarding/indoctrination drip series (Delivery, Origin, Myth Busting, Proof, Offer).
3. **Abandoned Cart Emails**: 3-stage recovery flow with escalating urgency and dynamic incentives.
4. **Win-Back Campaigns**: 30/60/90-day re-engagement flows for lapsed customers.

---

- **Workflow**: `../workflows/email-lifecycle.md`
- **Memory**: `../memory/lifecycle-flows.md` + `../memory/frameworks.md`
