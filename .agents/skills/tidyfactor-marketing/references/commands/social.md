# Command: social

Runtime dispatcher for LinkedIn B2B Marketing, Instagram & TikTok Strategies, Social Audits, 0-to-1,000 Follower Playbooks, and Hashtags.

---

## Capabilities Handled

1. **LinkedIn & B2B Marketing**: Founder & executive personal branding, company page amplification, 4 B2B content pillars, and 3-touch outbound InMail etiquette.
2. **Instagram Strategy**: Content mix (Reels, Carousels, Stories), bio conversion, highlight anchors, posting cadence.
3. **TikTok Strategy**: Short-form video scripts, 0-3s hook taxonomies, pacing, and trend mechanics.
4. **Social Media Audit**: Profile teardown, engagement rate diagnostics, content leak identification.
5. **First 1,000 Followers**: $1.80 outbound engagement plan, peer collaboration flywheels, high-reach formats.
6. **Hashtag Strategy**: 3-Tier hashtag taxonomy (Broad, Niche, Micro/Branded).

---
