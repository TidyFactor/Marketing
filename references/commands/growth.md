# Command: growth

Runtime dispatcher for Customer Retention, Loyalty Programs, Referral Programs, Influencer Outreach, and Brand Awareness.

---

## Capabilities Handled

1. **Customer Retention & Churn Reduction**: Drop-off diagnosis by business model (SaaS/E-commerce/Services), churn prevention triggers, and 30/60/90-day win-back campaigns.
2. **Loyalty Program Design**: Architecture matched to business model (Points, Tiered Status, Cashback, or Perks-based).
3. **Referral Program**: Two-sided incentive mechanics (Give $X, Get $Y), gamified milestone tiers, touchpoint triggers.
4. **Influencer Outreach Plan**: Vetting scorecards, cold DM/email outreach templates, gifting vs commission contracts.
5. **Brand Awareness Plan**: Multi-channel organic syndication flywheels, podcast pitching, co-marketing partnerships.
