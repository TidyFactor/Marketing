# Command: advertising

Runtime dispatcher for Direct Response Ad Copy, Landing Page Strategy, 7-Dimension CRO Audits, Meta Ads, Google Ads, and First Ad Campaigns.

---

## Capabilities Handled

1. **Write Ad Copy**: Multi-variation ad copy matrices (Loss Aversion, Logic/ROI, Status angles) with strict anti-cliché replacements.
2. **Landing Page Strategy & 7-Dimension CRO**: Above-the-fold hero architecture (5-second clarity test), wireframe messaging hierarchy, objection busters, and mobile form optimization.
3. **Facebook / Meta Ads Plan**: Advantage+ vs ABO architectures, creative testing frameworks, scaling rules.
4. **Google Ads Plan**: 15-Headline RSA structure, search intent alignment, negative keyword lists.
5. **First Ad Campaign**: $10-$20/day low-risk validation setups, pixel verification, stop-loss triggers.

---
