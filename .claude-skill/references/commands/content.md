# Command: content

Runtime dispatcher for Social Media Posts, SEO Content Strategy, Content Calendars, and Newsletter Blueprints.

---

## Capabilities Handled

1. **Social Media Content**: High-engagement multi-platform posts with native hooks and direct CTAs.
2. **SEO Strategy & Pillar-Cluster**: Keyword search-intent mapping (Informational/Commercial/Transactional), comprehensive pillar pages, programmatic SEO templates, and long-tail cluster articles.
3. **Email Campaign Copy**: One-off broadcast newsletters and promotional email copy.
4. **Content Calendar**: 30-day weekly/monthly publishing grids across channels with format specifications.
5. **Newsletter Strategy**: Formats, content pillars, monetization models, and growth tactics.

---

- **Workflow**: `../workflows/content-engine.md`
- **Memory**: `../memory/frameworks.md` + `../memory/platform-specs.md`
